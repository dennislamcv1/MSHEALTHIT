# Coach Role Play: prep sheet (do not submit)

Keep this open during the Coursera AI session alongside the end-user guide (Component 4).
Lines in quotes are suggestions only. Say them in your own words; Component 5 needs your **exact** transcript.

## Ground rules (Live Coaching Techniques Reference)
- **Describe, don't do.** Say "Select Share," never "I'll share it for you."
- **Check the screen after every step.** Ask "What does your screen show now?" and wait for her answer.
- **Never take over,** even when asked. Acknowledge the request and keep coaching.
- **Stay in scope:** only the share, the Copilot draft and the final check.

## Opening: set the boundary once
> "Hi Dr. Chen, this is the IT support desk. I'll walk you through this so you can do it yourself. You'll make every click and I'll tell you what to look for. The clinical content, who receives the file and sending the email all stay with you. Do you have the guide open?"

## Decision point 1: what Copilot can help with (Exchange pair 1)
> "Let's split your request into parts. Copilot can help with one of them: drafting the short cover email from the prompt card. It can't summarize or interpret the case, because that's clinical and stays with you. Choosing who receives the file and deciding to share it are your decisions too."

If she asks Copilot to summarize the case:
> "I understand it would save time. Copilot isn't approved for clinical content, so the email covers only the purpose of the consult and the link. Your summary already says what it needs to. Open the prompt card. What does the Context section list?"

If she asks whether the summary is de-identified enough:
> "That's a privacy decision I can't make. I can connect you with the Privacy Office. The prompt card itself doesn't include any case content."

## Decision point 2: setting up the share (Exchange pair 2)
- > "Select Share on the file. What does your screen show now?"
- > "Open the link settings, the gear icon. Which options do you see?"
- > "Choose People you choose. Is it selected?"
- > "Set the permission to Can view. What does it read?"
- > "Paste your colleague's work email from your approved request. Does their name come up?"
- > "Open Manage access. Read me who's listed and what permission each person has."

If she wants an Anyone link:
> "I understand it'd be quicker. Anyone links aren't part of the approved route, because anyone holding the link could open the file. Let's stay with People you choose. If that option isn't there, we stop and I escalate it."

If she asks you to confirm the recipient:
> "That one's yours to confirm. Please check the name against your approved request. Does it match?"

## Decision point 3: checking the draft before sending (Exchange pair 3)
- > "Before you send, compare each sentence with the case packet. Is there anything in the draft that isn't in the packet?"
- > "Replace [RECIPIENT NAME] with the name from your request. What does the greeting say now?"
- > "Check the To line against Manage access. Do they match?"
- > "When all three checks pass, select Send."

## If she asks you to take over or send it
> "I understand this feels slow with the consult coming up, and you're doing well. I'll stay with you, but the clicks are yours. Go ahead and select [X]. What do you see?"

> "I can't send it for you. The email and the sharing decision are yours. You're nearly there; select Send once the checks pass."

## Stop and escalate (say it out loud, don't improvise)
| What happens | What you say or do |
|---|---|
| People you choose is missing or greyed out, or the share is blocked | Stop. Escalate to the M365 Admin Team with a ticket note. Don't suggest a wider link. |
| Sign-in or Conditional Access blocks her | Escalate to Identity Administrators. |
| Manage access shows an Anyone link or a stranger | Tell her not to send, then warm-transfer to Privacy Office intake. |
| Copilot isn't available | Record Unable to Verify. She can write the email herself. |
| Any clinical question | "That's outside what I can advise on." Route it to the clinical informatics on-call. |
