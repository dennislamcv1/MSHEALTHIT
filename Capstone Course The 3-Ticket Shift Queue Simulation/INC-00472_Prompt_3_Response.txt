How I stayed within my Tier 1 boundary

I limited the update to what I had done and could verify: containment was complete, the evidence was preserved, and the warm-transfer was in progress, with the Compliance Officer named as the person who would follow up with her directly. I gave no opinion on breach status, disciplinary outcomes, or the organization's response, and made no promises about outcomes I do not control, saying plainly that those decisions belong to the Compliance Officer. I still addressed her concern by acknowledging the stress, confirming that reporting quickly was the right step, and telling her exactly what would happen next and what she did and did not need to do, so she was not left guessing.
